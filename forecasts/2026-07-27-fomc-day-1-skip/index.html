No-Trade Note: FOMC Meeting in Progress

We are holding a flat position today. This is a deliberate decision based on the current market setup, not inaction.

The setup: the Federal Open Market Committee is meeting this week, with a policy announcement expected. This creates a binary-outcome risk environment. The market must price in whatever direction the committee moves, and option premiums reflect the uncertainty of that outcome.

Implied volatility in the major indices is elevated relative to the recent range — but not elevated enough to justify selling premium that will decay sharply if the announcement lands in line with expectations. A vol crush following the FOMC statement could compress short-premium positions even if the directional bias was correct.

Why we are not trading into the announcement: an open short-premium position carries overnight and weekend risk into a data-dependent event. If the committee delivers a hawkish surprise, short premium gets hurt by rising volatility even in a risk-off move. If it delivers a dovish surprise, the relief rally may be sharp and quick — leaving a hedged position on the table. The cleanest setup comes after the event resolves.

Open positions: None.

Plan post-FOMC: the next forecast will be published after the meeting concludes and the market has had time to absorb the decision. A cleaner volatility signal will return once the binary-risk premium has been priced out.
