Why Hold Flat Into the Decision

Wednesday's FOMC announcement is a known catalyst with binary outcome risk across two channels: the rate decision itself (cut / hold / hike — consensus is hold at 4.25–4.50% with a hawkish-skewed dot plot) and Powell's press conference guidance (forward commentary on the September meeting, balance sheet, and any surprise language on inflation or employment).

If the dot plot shifts hawkish (signaling fewer 2026 cuts), the front-month VIX spikes 3–5 points in the announcement window, the term structure crushes the long end, and any short premium position is marked down even if the strike holds. If the dot plot shifts dovish, the long end re-rates higher on growth optimism but the front end crushes sharply — a short premium position realizes the gain but is already locked out of the larger directional follow-through.

In both cases, the open position captures only a fraction of the move and gives up the optionality of waiting for cleaner pricing. The risk-adjusted approach is to wait for the catalyst to resolve, then reassess with a fresh vol regime and a cleaner signal.
