Catalyst setup

The next hours are focused entirely on the Jackson Hole symposium.

Jackson Hole Symposium (Aug 21-23, Wyoming): The Federal Reserve's annual gathering begins today. Fed Chair remarks are expected mid-to-late afternoon ET — typically 1:00-3:00 PM ET, with the exact timing announced by the Fed the morning of. Markets are currently pricing approximately 65% probability of a 25bp rate cut at the Sep 17-18 FOMC meeting. The two data points that determine whether that cut is delivered (August jobs report Sep 4, August CPI Sep 10) are not in this week's calendar. The Jackson Hole symposium sets the tone for the Fed's reaction function ahead of those data releases.

A dovish speech (emphasizing global risks, labor market caution, or financial conditions concerns) would support risk assets and potentially weaken the dollar — positive for equities. A hawkish speech (emphasizing inflation vigilance or economic resilience) would tighten financial conditions and pressure equity multiples, particularly tech given its rate-sensitivity. With VIX at 15.72 and breadth at 75% (down from 100%), the market is positioned defensively enough that any surprise in either direction could produce a sharper-than-1σ reaction.

Pre-event positioning: The market has been selling into the Jackson Hole event over the past five days — SPY -1.96%, QQQ -2.89%, IWM -1.92% on the 5-day. This pre-event de-risking is the typical pattern ahead of major Fed communications. The asymmetric positioning means that a dovish surprise could produce an outsized positive reaction (overshoot upside as shorts cover and defensive positioning unwinds), while a hawkish surprise would confirm the bearish positioning and likely extend the pullback. This is a two-way event with both tail risks present.

Fed Blackout: The Fed has been in official blackout since Thursday Aug 20 ahead of Jackson Hole. Fed officials are prohibited from public commentary on monetary policy until after the symposium concludes. The market has traded on economic data and any incremental headlines without the benefit of Fed guidance. This makes today's keynote even more significant as the next real Fed communication.

September FOMC path: The two data releases between now and the Sep 17-18 FOMC — Aug NFP (Sep 4) and Aug CPI (Sep 10) — will determine whether the 25bp cut is delivered. A +165K to +200K jobs print with stable wage growth would keep the cut probability elevated. A hotter CPI or stronger jobs number would reduce it.
